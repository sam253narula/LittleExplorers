Privacy policy

A clear explanation of what stays on your device, how Apple handles purchases and how to contact us.

Publisher: Samarth Narula
Contact: samarthnarula253@gmail.com
Policy date: 26 October 2026

1. Children and the game

Little Explorers is designed for young children with a parent or guardian. The game does not ask children for names, birth dates, email addresses, accounts or profiles. It has no chat, advertising, third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. It does not request access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.

2. Data stored on your device

Earned-star totals and the sound preference are saved locally. The game does not send gameplay progress to the publisher or operate a separate cloud-save service. A grown-up can reset stars in Parent Corner. Removing the app's data removes local progress and settings. Your device/platform backup settings may preserve copies under the platform's own controls.

3. Apple purchases

Apple processes the optional one-time premium purchase through the App Store. StoreKit provides product information and verifies ownership. The app does not receive payment-card details or bank credentials and does not send transaction identifiers to a publisher server. Resetting stars does not cancel ownership. Apple manages purchase history, payment and refund requests under Apple's privacy policy. The publisher may access sales and financial reports through Apple's developer services; this game does not build a child purchase profile or combine those reports with gameplay.

4. Speech and sound

Prompts use installed system speech voices where available, with bundled audio as a fallback. There is no microphone recording or publisher-operated speech service.

5. Parent controls

Purchase, restore and settings controls are behind a parent challenge. This is a child deterrent, not identity verification or consent to collect a child's data. The game does not operate a child-data consent flow. Website links are reserved for the parent area.

6. Support and privacy requests

If a parent or guardian emails us, we receive the email address and message contents they choose to send. We use that information only to provide support and handle privacy requests. We do not use support emails for advertising or sell them. Please do not send children's personal information.

Support uses Gmail, provided by Google. We delete resolved conversations from the support mailbox within 30 days unless retention is required by law. Contact us to request access, correction or earlier deletion. Google's own infrastructure retention and backup practices may apply; see Google's privacy policy. We may retain a record when needed to meet a legal obligation.

7. This website and hosting

This parent website contains information and email links. We have not added analytics scripts, advertising pixels, contact forms, accounts or a checkout. Opening an email link uses your own email app and provider.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not a visitor is signed into GitHub. The public website does not require a GitHub account. This hosting processing is separate from the game's on-device progress. GitHub controls its service logs, security and retention under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Deleting a support email does not delete records controlled by GitHub.

8. Changes and contact

We will update the policy date and the published/in-app policy when practices change. Online accounts, analytics, advertising, cloud saves or new data services require a fresh privacy assessment before an affected game update is distributed. For questions, requests or concerns, email samarthnarula253@gmail.com.
