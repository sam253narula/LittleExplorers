Parent support

Questions about the game, a purchase or your child's stars? Start here.

Please include your app version, iPhone/iPad model, iOS version and a short description of the issue. Send messages as a parent or guardian. Don't include a child's name, photos, voice, Apple Account password, payment-card details or identity documents.

Release status: the game is currently in development and local testing. These instructions describe the planned free + premium edition.

What is free, and what does premium unlock?

Rainbow Garden is free, with unlimited six-round sessions. A one-time nonconsumable purchase unlocks Shape Safari, Animal Friends and Counting Stars. There is no subscription. The planned India launch price is ₹99; the actual price will be displayed by Apple.

How do I restore a premium purchase?
  1. Use the Apple Account that made the purchase.
  2. Open Little Explorers and go to Parents' corner.
  3. Solve the displayed grown-up challenge.
  4. Tap Restore purchases with an internet connection.

A local Xcode test purchase is separate from an App Store purchase and cannot be transferred into a public release. Never buy premium again merely to restore it.

Why is premium purchase unavailable?

Check the App Store connection, your Apple Account and purchase restrictions. In Parent Corner, try Retry App Store connection. Rainbow Garden remains available. If it continues, email support with the details above.

Can children reach purchase controls?

Purchase, restore and settings controls are behind a typed grown-up challenge. The adult session expires and closes when the app backgrounds. The challenge is a child deterrent, not identity verification. Apple's own purchase authorization still applies.

Does the game work offline?

The learning activities are bundled with the app. Purchasing and restoring require an App Store connection. Installed device speech voices are preferred; bundled audio is available as a fallback. Exact voices vary by device.

How do I turn sound off or reset stars?

Open Parents' corner, solve the grown-up challenge and use Sound: Off or Reset all earned stars. Resetting stars cannot be undone and does not remove purchase ownership. The game does not offer a separate cloud-save service.

How do I request a refund?

Apple handles App Store payment and refund requests. Visit Apple's Report a Problem service. Apple determines eligibility; support cannot guarantee a refund or access your Apple payment details.

How are support emails handled?

We use messages only to provide support and address privacy requests. Support is handled through Gmail. Resolved conversations are deleted from the support mailbox within 30 days unless retention is required by law. Please ask us if you want a conversation deleted sooner. Google's own service retention and backup practices also apply.
